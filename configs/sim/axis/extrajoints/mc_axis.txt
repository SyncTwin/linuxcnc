mc_axis demo

The extra joint 4 is managed by an
mc_axis component (PLCopen MC Part 1
axis) after homing, instead of limit3.

Usage:
1) F1        (Estop Off)
2) F2        (Machine On)
3) Ctrl-HOME (Home all)
4) In sim_pin set j4.position
   (-10..10) and press j4.execute:
   the joint moves, j4.done is set
   when it arrives.
5) j4.mode 1 = relative, 2 = velocity
   (j4.velocity signed).
6) j4.halt stops a move; a target
   outside the limits is refused
   (j4.error, j4.error-id 5);
   j4.reset clears an axis error.
7) j4.state shows the PLCopen state
   (1 Standstill, 3 Discrete motion...).
